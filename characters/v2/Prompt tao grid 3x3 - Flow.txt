## Prompt

Use the provided reference image as the **exact character reference**.

Transform the static character into a **9-frame sequential animation sprite sheet** showing one continuous, smooth movement.

### Animation requirements

* Create **exactly 9 sequential frames** arranged in a **3×3 grid**.
* The 9 frames must depict **one continuous action/movement**, progressing naturally from frame 1 → frame 9.
* Keep the **same movement direction throughout all 9 frames**.
* Keep the **same camera angle and camera orientation** throughout all frames.
* Keep the **same character scale and proportions** throughout all frames.
* The character must remain **fixed at the center of each grid cell**.
* The character's overall position must NOT shift from cell to cell.
* Maintain consistent framing, perspective, camera distance, and viewing angle.
* Preserve the character's exact identity, appearance, clothing, colors, proportions, hairstyle, accessories, and visual style from the reference image.
* Only the **pose and body movement** should change between frames.
* Movement should be subtle and fluid, with natural intermediate poses between key poses.
* Each frame must connect smoothly to the previous and next frame.
* Avoid sudden jumps, teleportation, deformation, or inconsistent poses.
* Keep the character fully inside its individual cell.
* The character should occupy approximately the **same percentage of the cell area** in every frame.

### Grid layout

Create a clean **3×3 sprite sheet**:

Frame 1 | Frame 2 | Frame 3
Frame 4 | Frame 5 | Frame 6
Frame 7 | Frame 8 | Frame 9

Each frame must occupy exactly one equal-sized cell.

**Important:** The grid cells must be perfectly aligned and evenly sized. Do not overlap frames.

### Background

* **Transparent background (alpha channel).**
* No floor.
* No shadow.
* No environment.
* No scenery.
* No props.
* No particles.
* No additional objects.
* No text.
* No UI elements.
* No borders or decorative elements.

### Character consistency

The reference character is the single source of truth.

Do NOT redesign, reinterpret, restyle, or modify the character.

Do NOT change:

* character identity
* body proportions
* face
* hairstyle
* clothing
* colors
* accessories
* materials
* camera perspective
* viewing direction
* character size

Only animate the character's body pose and movement.

### Critical constraints

**FIXED CHARACTER POSITION:**
The character must be centered in the exact same location within every grid cell.

**FIXED CAMERA:**
The camera is completely locked. No zoom, pan, rotation, tilt, perspective change, or camera movement.

**FIXED SCALE:**
The character has exactly the same scale in every frame.

**FIXED DIRECTION:**
The character continuously moves in the same direction throughout the entire animation.

**CONTINUOUS MOTION:**
Frame 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 must form a smooth animation cycle/sequence with consistent motion spacing.

Generate the final result as **one single 3×3 sprite sheet image containing exactly 9 frames**.
